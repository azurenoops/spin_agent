## Exchange Online collaboration mail | AC-2, SC-7

Provider accountable: Maintain documented mail-flow defaults, operator access controls and service-impact notifications for the demo boundary.

Joint handoff: Review approved external mail domains and investigate delivery-policy exceptions with tenant administrators.

Customer accountable: Approve mailboxes, review distribution groups, label messages and enforce mission-specific handling restrictions.

Acceptance artifact: M365-E01: synthetic mail-flow review and mailbox access sample. Review cadence: monthly and after material change. Customer sign-off is required before claiming applicability; an unresolved customer duty remains customer-owned.
